SimBLE Ranging

RSSI path loss versus Bluetooth 6.0 Channel Sounding, against a known truth
SimBLE compiled to WebAssembly. Two devices share one in-page simulated radio: a tag serving the Ranging Service (0x185B) and a locator running the Channel Sounding procedure against it. The RSSI comes from HCI Read RSSI; the tones come from LE CS Subevent Result events and from RAS Ranging Data notified over ATT. Nothing here is computed from the slider.
