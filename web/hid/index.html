HID over GATT

a simulated Bluetooth keyboard and mouse, and a computer decoding their input reports
SimBLE compiled to WebAssembly. Four devices — two HOGP peripherals and two centrals — share one in-page simulated radio; no netsim and no external process. HID Service 0x1812, Report Map 0x2A4B, Report 0x2A4D, Protocol Mode 0x2A4E.
