SimBLE compiled to WebAssembly. An Auracast broadcast source and its listeners: a Broadcast Audio Announcement (0x1852) on an extended advertising set, the BASE (0x1851) on a periodic advertising train, BIGInfo in that train's ACAD, and a BIG carrying one LC3 stream per BIS. No connection and no pairing exists anywhere on this page. On netsim the devices join as web-auracast-src and web-auracast-rx1…4; netsim is the only controller here, because the in-page radio models neither periodic advertising nor a BIG.
